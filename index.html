Getting Started

The Tootsie Roll Program Report is an online form used by Wisconsin KofC councils to report the results of their annual Tootsie Roll Drive — the fundraiser that supports individuals with intellectual disabilities in our communities. Councils use this form to submit their collection totals, program participation details, and related information to the State Council.
This form should be completed by the council officer or program director responsible for the Tootsie Roll Drive — typically the Grand Knight, Financial Secretary, or the designated Program Director. One submission per council is expected.
The form is publicly accessible at:

https://kocs-new-dev-wa.azurewebsites.net/TootsieRollProgramForm
No login is required to access or submit the form.
Before opening the form, gather the following:
  • Your council number (e.g., 499, 524, etc.)
  • Total amount of money collected during the drive
  • Program Director name and his contact information
  • Organizations that your council wishes to donate to — the EIN number is required for each organization, along with the amount you wish to donate to each
Having this information ready before you start will allow you to complete the form in one session.

Filling Out the Form

Enter the total dollar amount of all monetary donations collected during your council's Tootsie Roll Drive. This includes cash and coin collected at all locations and shifts.
  • Do not include the cost or value of Tootsie Roll candy purchased.
  • Round to the nearest dollar if needed.
  • If multiple Knights collected separately, combine all totals before entering.
List the name(s) of the local organizations, programs, or individuals that received funds from your Tootsie Roll Drive. Examples include group homes, special education programs, disability service nonprofits, or diocesan programs. If funds have not yet been distributed, list the intended recipients.
Required fields are typically marked with an asterisk (*). Optional fields provide additional context and are encouraged but not mandatory. Complete as many fields as possible to give the State Council a full picture of your council's program activity.
Make sure to click the "Update" button directly after changing the information in the top section. The "Total Collected" field will not have a dollar sign — enter only a number in decimal format.
Example: Enter 1234.78 if you collected $1,234.78.

Please check Officers Online to determine who is assigned as your council's Program Director. Use that individual's name in the Program Director field on the form.

Submitting the Form

After clicking the submit button, you should see a confirmation message on the screen indicating your report was received. If you provided an email address, a confirmation email may also be sent.
If you do not see a confirmation message, do not submit again — contact your District Deputy or the State Office to verify receipt.
The form does not currently support saving a draft. We recommend gathering all required information before starting so you can complete it in one session. The form typically takes less than 10 minutes to complete when you have your data ready.
Do not submit the form a second time. Instead, contact your District Deputy or the Wisconsin State Council office as soon as possible and provide the correct information. Corrections can be made on the administrative side.
The deadline is communicated by the State Council each year. Check your council communications, the wikofc.org website, or contact your District Deputy for the current year's deadline. Late submissions may not be counted in state totals.
The following people automatically receive a copy of the submitted form:
  • Grand Knight (GK)
  • Council Program Director
  • District Deputy (DD)
  • State ID Program Director
  • State Administrative Manager
Please reach out to one of the above individuals to request a copy.

Technical Issues

Try the following steps:
  • Refresh the page and try again.
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies, then reload.
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge).
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
If the issue persists, contact the State Council office and describe the error you're seeing.
First, make sure all required fields (marked with *) are filled in. Look for any red error messages near specific fields. If everything appears correct and the form still won't submit, take a screenshot of the page and contact your District Deputy or the State Office for help.
The form is accessible on mobile devices, but for the best experience we recommend completing it on a desktop or laptop computer where you can easily reference your collected data.
